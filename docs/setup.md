# Preparing the session

To prepare this training optimally, please 
1. Download the [example data](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24033765),
2. retrive an API-Key to Blablador as explained on [this page](https://sdlaml.pages.jsc.fz-juelich.de/ai/guides/blablador_api_access/).
3. [backup-option](https://datashare.tu-dresden.de/s/LgxPBoJgRQYeCdy) (in case the above doesn't work)

Afterwards, you have two options for proceeding: 

1. [setting everything up in the Jupyter4NFDI cloud](setup_jup_nfdi.md), or
2. [installing software on your computer](setup_python_locally.md).
